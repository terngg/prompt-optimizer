# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different experience levels, give constructive feedback, and focus disagreements on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sharing private information without permission, and persistent disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or ban repeat offenders proportionally to the conduct. Decisions should be explained privately where feasible and applied consistently. Report concerns privately to terngg at albinghx@gmail.com. Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue.

These expectations apply in project issues, reviews, discussions and other official community spaces.
